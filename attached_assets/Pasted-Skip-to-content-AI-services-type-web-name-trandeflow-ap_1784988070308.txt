Here's exactly what happens step by step when you set 10% and press START AUTO BOT:

1. Scan (every 5 seconds) The bot pulls the last 30 one-minute candles for BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT from MEXC and scores each one against 8 conditions:

RSI ≤ 55 (not overbought)
Last candle is green (bullish)
3 of last 5 candles are green
Candle body size maintained
More wick below than above (buying pressure)
Price above the 10-candle moving average
Higher low than previous candle
Sustained higher lows (trend)
2. Signal threshold Needs 6 out of 8 to pass. If neither BTC nor ETH scores 6+, it waits 5 seconds and scans again — you'll see "Signal X/8 — need 6/8, scanning again…" on screen.

3. Real buy on MEXC Once a signal passes, it calculates your stake: 1.86 USDT × 10% = 0.19 USDT and places a real market BUY on MEXC — actual crypto is purchased from your account.

4. Monitor the position (up to 30 seconds) Every 2 seconds it checks the live price and re-scores the signal. It exits early if:

Price rises +0.3% → exits as WIN
Price drops -0.3% → exits as LOSS
Signal drops below 6/8 → exits immediately
30 seconds pass → exits regardless
5. Real sell on MEXC Places a market SELL of the exact crypto amount bought. USDT profit/loss lands back in your account. Result is recorded in trade history.

6. Loop Immediately starts scanning again for the next signal.

At 10% of 1.86 USDT, each trade stakes about 0.19 USDT — small enough to run many cycles without draining the account fast. Raise the % to trade larger per cycle.
