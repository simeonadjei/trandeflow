How sure is this bot to win per trade

Honest answer: no bot can guarantee wins, and you should know exactly what you're working with before putting money in.

Here's the reality:

What the golden window actually tells you The bot found the 1-hour slot where BTC/ETH historically closed up more often than down over the last 30 days. If the best hour had a 73% win rate, that means 73 out of 100 past 1h candles at that hour closed green. That's historical pattern, not a prediction guarantee.

Why past win rates don't equal future wins

Crypto markets shift constantly — a pattern that held for 30 days can flip in a week
The 30-day window is short; a bull run skews every hour bullish
MEXC market orders have fees and slippage that eat into small gains
Your TP is +0.8%, SL is -0.4%. Even with 65% wins, if losses average 0.4% and wins average 0.8%, you need more than 33% win rate just to break even
What the 12 indicators actually add Each indicator is a pattern check — if 10/12 agree UP before trading, you're filtering out weak setups. Historically that raises the odds of a winning entry, but it doesn't eliminate losing trades. Good signals still lose during volatile or trending-against-you conditions.

Realistic expectation A well-calibrated bot like this, trading at the statistically favorable hour with strict indicator filtering, might win 55–70% of trades over time — which, with your 0.8% TP / 0.4% SL ratio, is profitable. But individual trades are never "sure." Any single trade can lose.

The real risk with your current setup You have ~$5 USDT. MEXC fees (~0.2% per side = 0.4% round trip) leave almost nothing from a 0.8% gain. With $5 stake, your best-case win is about $0.02 after fees. The system works better with $50–$500+ where fees become a smaller percentage.

Bottom line: the bot is genuinely trying to find the highest-probability moment to trade — and it's real logic, not fake — but "90% sure to win" is not possible with any bot. Treat it as a tool that tilts the odds in your favor, not a sure thing.
